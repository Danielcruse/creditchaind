02Block reward — new mechanism

Replaced entirely on 2026-08-25, per explicit instruction: the reward is no longer a flat or decaying schedule. It's now 90% of whatever real CCT was burned from marketplace fees since the last block.

ConstantFileValueNotes
Reward formulaapi/apply.go90% × CCTBurnedPendingRewardpendingBurnBasedBlockReward(), paid and reset every block.
Accumulatorcore/treasury.goCCTBurnedPendingRewardFed only by the CCT-denominated half of the platform fee's 50% burn share.
Worked example — a 10,000 CCT trade
10,000 CCTTrade amount
→
30 CCTPlatform fee (0.3%)
→
15 CCTBurned (50% of fee)
→
13.5 CCTMinted as reward (90%)

Net effect per fee-burn cycle: total supply falls by 10% of the burned amount, and the validator (split pro‑rata with delegators, exactly as before) receives the other 90% as newly minted CCT. A block with zero CCT-denominated fee activity since the last one now correctly pays zero reward — the old schedule always paid something regardless of real usage.

Needs decision — no volume floor

With no CCT trading volume, the block reward is exactly zero — the only validator income left is swept CCT transfer fees (0.3 CCT/send). Is a zero base reward acceptable during low-activity periods, or should there be a minimum floor?

Resolved (2026-08-25): ETH_CCC1/BTC_CCC1/USDC_CCC1 burns now feed the reward too. Each is valued in CCT terms — via a new admin-settable CctUsdCents governance param, chained through the existing EthUsdCentsPerEth/BtcUsdCentsPerBtc rates (USDC_CCC1 pegged 1:1) — and that valuation is added to the same CCTBurnedPendingReward accumulator real CCT burns feed. Nothing is actually minted, burned, or moved across token ledgers to do this — each symbol's own burn still happens in its own currency exactly as before; this only changes how it's counted toward the reward. Deliberately does not read api/price_oracle.go's live, node-local price cache — that's independently polled per node and would fork the chain if used in block-apply logic. REPT still doesn't feed the reward (no defined USD/CCT rate exists for it anywhere). All three rates are 0 (no effect) until an admin sets them via the existing governance-param mechanism.
Needs decision — no smoothing or cap

The reward is paid raw, per block, with no smoothing or ceiling. A single large CCT trade produces one outsized single-block reward rather than an averaged rate. Worth deciding whether to smooth across a rolling window, or leave it exactly proportional to real-time activity.

Needs decision — self-dealing check

Worth a dedicated engineering look at wash-trading incentives: a validator trading CCT against themself pays the full 0.3% fee and can recover at most ~20% (validator pool share) + ~13.5% (90% of the burn, if they produced the block) ≈ 33.5% of that fee back — a net loss under the current splits, so no obvious profit today, but flagging for explicit sign-off rather than assuming it's safe.

Removed from the codebase: the flat/decaying schedule this replaced (Year 1–5: 10 → 8 → 6 → 5 → 3 CCT/block, held flat thereafter) and its ProduceBlockReward/blockRewardSchedule/blockRewardForTimestamp constants no longer exist. Full before/after is documented in audit/economic_constants_recommendations.md §3 and audit/KNOWN_GAPS.md.

06Validator staking & registration economics

The capital and fee cost of standing up a new validator.

ConstantFileValueNotes
MinValidatorStakecore/consensus.go1,000 CCT1% of StakeCap → only 0.25% of max selection weight.

MinValidatorReputationapi/validators.go50Matches a fresh wallet's default — immediately eligible.
Registration scalingapi/validators.goflat / 10 / +10%Flat for the first 10 validators, then +10% (fee & stake) per additional block of 10.
Needs decision — on file, unactioned

A brand-new, minimum-stake validator reaches ~48% of maximum selection weight (0.25% stake + up to 27.5% reputation + 20% uptime) for ~1,050 CCT and zero track record. Standing recommendation: raise MinValidatorStake to 10,000 CCT (10% of StakeCap) and ValidatorRegisterFeeCCT to 500 CCT to match. Full Sybil-cost derivation in economic_constants_recommendations.md §2.

Needs decision — on file, unactioned

The 10-validators / +10% registration-scaling curve is a reasonable shape but an arbitrary pair without a target active validator-set size (curated ~180–1,000 vs. permissionless 1,000+). Needs owner input on which model CreditChain is aiming for.
