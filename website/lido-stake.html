Stake ETH

Stakes your real vault-locked ETH through Lido, an established, audited liquid-staking protocol - CreditChain never runs its own validator or holds a staking key. Three separate real transactions, each one you sign and confirm yourself: unvault (get real ETH back to your own wallet), stake on Lido (receive real stETH, which grows over time as staking rewards accrue), and lock into the vault (mint STETH_CCC1 you can use on CreditChain, e.g. as loan collateral). You hold the real ETH/stETH in your own wallet at every point in between - nothing is stuck if you stop partway through.
