Sponsored Posts

What This Is

Pay CCT to promote a post into the governance feed - either to everyone, or scoped to one validator's own community (every wallet currently delegating CCT to that validator). A moderator reviews every post before it goes live; nothing is ever shown unreviewed.

How much CCT you may spend on a single post is capped by your RC (REPT) balance - build real reputation to unlock a larger campaign. The fee itself is split between burning, the targeted validator (or the governance fund for a global post), the Foundation Treasury, and the moderator pool, per a split the community votes on through governance - see Governance.
