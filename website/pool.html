Liquidity Pool

Every token on this network - native (ETHTEST, USDCTEST) or developer-registered via Token Creation - trades against CCT through a constant-product pool, the same pricing model a real DEX uses. CCT is the hub currency: swapping between two non-CCT tokens automatically routes through both pools as two hops.
