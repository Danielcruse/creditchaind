Welcome back. This browser already has a wallet.



This app is secured by a wallet keypair, not a password - there's no account to "log in" to until you create one.



Restore With Backup Phrase

Paste the 12-word BIP-39 phrase you saved at registration.



Your backup phrase is the only way to restore a wallet and its derived ETH/BTC self-custodial addresses. There is no other recovery option - not email, not support, nothing. If it's lost, the wallet and anything in it are unrecoverable, the same as any real self-custody crypto wallet. Save it somewhere safe when you first create your account.

Sign In With Private Key

Paste your CreditChain wallet's raw private key (hex) instead - either the full 64-byte key or just the 32-byte seed.



This only restores your CreditChain wallet itself - it can't re-derive any ETH/BTC self-custodial addresses (those come from the full 12-word phrase, not this key alone). Use the backup phrase above instead if you need those back too.
