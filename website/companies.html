What This Is

A directory of sellers who applied for a "Verified" badge and were reviewed by a registered moderator.

Testnet placeholder - not real KYC/AML. This is a stand-in for a real identity-verification and compliance system that will be built before mainnet (real licensing, a real KYC/AML vendor, legal review). Right now, "Verified" means a moderator read what an applicant wrote and vouched for it - nothing here is independently verified, no real documents are collected, and no real-world fiat moves through this app. Do not treat a badge here as a real compliance guarantee.
