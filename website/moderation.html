Moderation

What This Is

Registered moderators are automatically assigned to observe new trade agreements (peer-funded loan negotiations and P2P offers) and attest on chain whether they concluded successfully.

This is a disclosed, human-trust-based attestation, not a cryptographic guarantee that any off-app payment actually happened - a moderator's confirmation is their own judgment call, recorded transparently, never an automatic release or reversal of funds. Trades already settle on their own (a negotiation executes when the lender confirms, a P2P offer settles atomically on accept) - moderation adds an extra trust layer on top, it doesn't gate the trade itself.

Requires reputation ≥ 50 (same floor as becoming a validator), at least 200 CCT already staked (locked as a moderation bond on registration), and a flat 10 CCT registration fee, charged when the registration confirms. Registering grants a one-time reputation bump, which also raises your validator selection weight (reputation is 55% of it). Both the fee and the stake requirement are governable - admin-settable directly, executive-vote settable, or changeable by any ordinary community Parameter Change vote if no executive consensus forms - so the numbers above are defaults, not guarantees.
