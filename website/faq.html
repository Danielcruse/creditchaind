Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions testers ask most. For the full protocol architecture, see the whitepaper.

What is CreditChain?

A live testnet blockchain combining Proof of Stake with an on-chain reputation score, so borrowing power depends on more than just how much you can lock up as collateral.

Is any of this real money?

No. CCT, ETHTEST, USDCTEST, and REPT are testnet participation tokens only. None of them are backed by, or convertible into, real currency or assets. See the disclaimer on the whitepaper for details.

Do I need an account or password?

No. Registering generates a real Ed25519 keypair in your browser; your wallet address is derived directly from your public key. Whoever holds the keypair is the account - there's no separate username/password system behind it.

What can I use as collateral to borrow?

Staked CCT or oETH (the receipt you get for staking ETHTEST) - never your liquid, spendable CCT. oETH itself can't be sent or transferred anywhere else; locking it as collateral here is its only use.

Can another website build on top of CreditChain?

Yes - every endpoint allows cross-origin requests, and the signed-request scheme is self-certifying, so no API key or registration with CreditChain itself is required. See the founder notes below for the design reasoning, and the whitepaper for the technical architecture.

Has this been audited?

An internal preliminary review has been completed, but no independent professional security audit has been performed yet. That engagement, plus separate legal/regulatory review, is planned as a follow-up step before anything here could move toward representing real value. See Security Disclosures for the current, full list of known open gaps.
