Display Currency

Choose which real-world currency your crypto balances are converted into (shown alongside the native amount) on your Assets pages. This is a display preference only, saved in this browser - it never changes any actual balance or on-chain rate.


How your ETH/BTC balance works

The ETH and BTC balances shown on your Assets page are backed 1:1 by real bridged funds - deposits to your real Ethereum/Bitcoin testnet address auto-credit here, and the balance moves instantly and for free within CreditChain (no fee, no delay) just like any other token here.

When you send and choose "ERC network" (for ETH) or "BTC network" (for BTC) instead of "CCT network," that's the one moment a real bridge action happens: CreditChain sends real ETH or BTC to the address you specify on the real network, and a small bridge fee applies to cover that. Choosing "CCT network" instead just moves the balance to another CreditChain wallet, free of that fee, same as sending any other token here.
