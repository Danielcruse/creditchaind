Settings




Wallet Backup

Your 12-word BIP-39 backup phrase - shown once, at registration - is the only way to restore this wallet on a new browser or device. There is no email recovery, no support-assisted recovery, no alternative. If it's lost, the wallet and everything in it are unrecoverable, the same as any real self-custody crypto wallet. CreditChain never stores it and cannot show it to you again - if you haven't saved it somewhere safe, do that now while you still have access.


Contact Email

Optional. Used only to notify you - e.g. a validator application confirmation link, a P2P trade starting, or a dispute needing your attention. This is not a recovery method: it never grants access to this wallet or anyone else's, and losing it changes nothing about your ability to sign in (your 12-word phrase is still the only key to that).






Display Currency

Choose which real-world currency your crypto balances are converted into (shown alongside the native amount) on your Assets pages. This is a display preference only, saved in this browser - it never changes any actual balance or on-chain rate.


How your ETH/BTC balance works

CreditChain never holds an ETH or BTC signing key on your behalf. The ETH and BTC balances shown on your Assets page are backed 1:1 by real funds sitting at your own self-custodial address (derived from your recovery phrase, right on this device) - deposits there auto-credit here, and the balance moves instantly and for free within CreditChain (no fee, no delay) just like any other token here.

When you send and choose "Direct" (for ETH) or "BTC network" (for BTC) instead of "CCT network," that's a real send: the transaction is signed entirely in your browser with your own key and broadcast straight to the real network - no CreditChain fee, only the real network gas cost. Choosing "CCT network" instead just moves the balance to another CreditChain wallet instantly, for a small flat fee, same as sending any other token here.
