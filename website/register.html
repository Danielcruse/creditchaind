Terms of Use

By creating an account you agree that OUTERNET bridges the crypto you deposit (ETH, BTC, and other supported assets) into CreditChain's own tradeable representations (e.g. ETHB, BTCB) so it can be used across the Crypto Marketplace (peer-to-peer trading) and the Loan Marketplace (borrowing and lending). Balances shown on CreditChain reflect these bridged representations, not a direct hold of the original asset on its native chain. This is a testnet/demo environment - see the site-wide banner for the current network's real-value status.
