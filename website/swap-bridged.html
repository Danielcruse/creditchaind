Swap

Fair-value conversion between real internal balances, priced off the live market rate (not a floating pool) - CCT is used only as the accounting unit shown below, your real value is protected either way. Any deposit that lands here (BTC, ETH) creates a real vault balance, and any vault balance can swap with any other, routed through CCT for pricing. Self-custodial ETH/USDC/BTC live on their own chains and have no CreditChain balance to move, so they're not part of this swap.

Trade ETH/BTC Through the Marketplace

Swapping between real vault-backed ETH and BTC happens through the Trading Hub marketplace's Native Token Swap - find a hub that offers the pair you want, and it settles instantly, atomically, straight from real balances (never newly minted). YEN-e trades still work below.
