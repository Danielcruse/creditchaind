Token Creation

Register a new testnet token here. A registered token has no (testnet) price against CCT until liquidity has been seeded for it - that happens automatically behind the scenes as part of the trades and settlements that use it, so there's nothing extra for you to do. Registering requires some test ETH staked first (same "skin in the game" requirement as borrowing) - see Stake if you haven't yet - plus a 5 ETHTEST creation fee, charged from your ETHTEST address (see your wallet card on OUTERNET).

Log in with a wallet to register a token.

You need some test ETH staked before you can register a token - stake some here first.

You need at least 5 ETHTEST in your ETHTEST address to cover the token creation fee - claim some from the faucet on OUTERNET first.
