Collateral

Collateral is locked from your STAKED CCT or your oETH balance (the receipt you got for staking ETHTEST) - never your liquid/spendable CCT. oETH itself is never transferable or sendable anywhere else in this app; locking it as collateral here is its only real use. Borrowed funds are paid out in USDCTEST. Repaying a loan returns the exact collateral that backed it to the exact same staked CCT / oETH balance it came from.
