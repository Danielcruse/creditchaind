Default Protection

If your OETH-collateralized loan's collateral value falls, its health indicator moves from Healthy to Safe to Warning. Once collateral value drops below 120% of what's owed, the next block's liquidation sweep seizes only the proportional slice of your collateral needed to cover the debt (never more), refunding the rest - a full grace period isn't modeled yet, so top up collateral early if you're at Warning. If liquidation still can't fully cover the lender, the shortfall reduces your reputation proportionally to how much was missed (never a flat penalty), and the lender's reputation gains the mirror amount. CCT-collateralized and self-funded loans have no live price feed, so they're due-date-only - they default (and forfeit 100% of collateral) only if not repaid by their due date, not on a price move.
