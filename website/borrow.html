Default Protection

We never punish immediately. If your OETH-collateralized loan's collateral value falls below 120% of what's owed (or a due-date loan goes overdue), your loan moves through a graduated sequence, each step giving you a real chance to fix it before anything is taken: Warning (an alert - add collateral or repay) → Margin Call (add collateral, repay part or all, or close voluntarily - your Mining Power is still untouched) → Grace Period (72 hours to act) → Liquidation (only once the grace period actually expires - seizes only the proportional slice of your collateral needed to cover the debt, never more, refunding the rest). If liquidation still can't fully cover the lender, you get one more chance - a 24-hour Final Redemption window to repay the exact shortfall and keep your Mining Power. Only if that window also expires does the shortfall reduce your Mining Power (scaled to how much was actually missed, never a flat penalty), with the lender's Mining Power gaining the mirror amount.
