Executive Ballots

You're currently one of the top-5 RC (REPT) holders - this network's live-computed governance "executives." Only executives may create or vote on this narrow ballot type, which is the sole way RealWorldTrustWeightBps (how much Infused real-world trust boosts mining power) ever changes. Open executive ballots appear above, in the Voting Ballots list.

Executives may also vote to deactivate a moderator who has abused their assigned role (the ledger of a moderator's own certificate-review decisions is executive-only - Trading Hub disputes are the usual reason). Deactivation is permanent: the moderator is removed from future trade-oversight assignment and can never re-register.

A public Yes/No prediction against a real, developer-accessible price pool. Anyone can attach RC to their vote to back a side with a real stake - the first RC-backed vote instantly forms a visible bet pool. At resolution, if the target was met the YES side wins (otherwise NO); winners split the losing side's pooled RC pro-rata to their own stake. A pool that stays open 14+ days mints a small bonus (up to 10% of the total pooled RC) shared across every infuser on both sides, regardless of outcome.






RC-backed votes cost 1 CCT (pays the validators processing the extra load) - a plain Yes/No vote with no RC attached stays free.

Reference a specific past decision by a moderator you believe abused the role - the moderator must have genuinely been assigned to that exact record, checked server-side before this proposal can even be created. If the vote passes, that moderator takes a real reputation penalty. This does not automatically refund any money - see the Trading Hub/marketplace dispute flow for that.





A proposal only takes effect if it reaches quorum (500 total voting weight) and passes with more Yes than No within its 7-day voting window - this is what makes a vote actually gate a critical decision, not just a poll. Proposing stays open to any activated wallet; the vote itself is the real gate.
