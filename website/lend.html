Post a Lend Offer

Post a standing offer to lend any token at a rate you choose - any borrower can discover it below, request an amount, and chat with you to finalize terms before anything is recorded on chain. Browsing the marketplace and negotiating as a borrower is open to everyone - only posting a lend offer requires the security pass + KYC below.
