Terms of the P2P Marketplace

These terms govern who may operate a Trading Hub on CreditChain's P2P Marketplace, and how identity verification and user data are handled when a Hub is involved. They apply to Hub operators specifically - ordinary peer-to-peer trading on the Marketplace (no Hub involved) is covered by the general Terms of Use shown at account creation.

1. Who May Operate a Trading Hub

Only users who are regulated and registered by their own government may apply to operate a Trading Hub. This exists so that each sovereign regulator can keep track of the taxable, documented trades conducted through a Hub under its jurisdiction. A Hub is a real-world operator doing business under real-world law - it is not an anonymous role.

2. Identity Verification and Where Data Lives

A Trading Hub is responsible for its own identity verification of the users who trade through it, answerable to its own jurisdiction's regulator. That verification information is stored only by the Hub - never by CreditChain, and never shared across Hubs.

This keeps user data restricted to, and within, a single jurisdiction: a nation's users' identity data stays with that nation's regulated Hub, under that nation's law, rather than pooling into one central, cross-border database.

3. CreditChain's Role

CreditChain only operates the underlying facilities - the Marketplace, escrow, matching, and on-chain settlement. CreditChain does not perform identity verification, does not hold user identity data, and does not act as a Hub itself. This keeps CreditChain the protocol fully decentralized: it is infrastructure that Hubs and traders use, not a party to the compliance relationship between a Hub and its regulator.
